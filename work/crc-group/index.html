The work

Context

CRC Group runs $800M+ in annual billing volume across 7,000 employer groups. Two parallel tracks ran across my time there. The first was a billing modernization program that needed to ship clean enough to support future M&A. The second is the current migration of two AI-enabled insurance platforms onto the CRC enterprise stack.

What I did

  • Cut 3+ months from the discovery-to-build timeline by running prototype-driven discovery directly with billing stakeholders, instead of the usual waterfall spec cycle.
  • Designed an M&A-ready billing architecture and identified $2M+ in cash-flow optimization through cleaner reconciliation workflows.
  • Program paused for a late-2026 restart; the prototype and requirements baseline are the restart assets.
  • Leading discovery and migration planning for two AI-enabled insurance platforms (submission intake + claims workflows) moving from an acquired platform to the CRC enterprise stack.
  • Decomposing undocumented workflows across six systems, from the CRM and document storage to identity checks and the API gateway.
  • Mapping system interactions to surface migration risk; aligning business stakeholders with engineering leads on scope and sequencing.

Outcomes

  • 3+ months saved on discovery-to-build timeline via prototype-driven discovery.
  • $2M+ cash-flow optimization identified through the billing architecture design.
  • Two AI platform migrations currently in discovery and planning: submission intake and claims workflows.
