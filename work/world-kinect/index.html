World Kinect Services

Sr. Business Analyst across the Land and Aviation fuel divisions, on the customer portal team of a two-year transformation program.

May 2016 to Nov 2021Miami, FLFive and a half years

The work

Context

World Kinect is a global fuel and logistics operator headquartered in Miami, moving billions of gallons a year across commercial land fleets and aviation customers in dozens of countries. The Land and Aviation divisions run very different operations (truck and depot logistics on one side, airport fueling on the other) against a shared ERP backbone and a common finance and compliance stack.

What I did

  • Analyzed customer-portal requirements with the product owner and worked with the UX designer and engineers on designs and refinement across the Billing, MDM and Logistics squads.
  • Requested and ran field research in the Kansas City pilot, including driver ride-alongs, which found that drivers had no input into requirements and dispatch was overriding optimized routes. Shared the findings with leadership, designers and engineers, reprioritized the backlog with product managers, and worked with the vendor and data teams on device and customer-data fixes.
  • Set up the first backlogs and agile rituals as the Aviation division moved from Waterfall to Agile, and worked with the delivery team replacing legacy Oracle 11i financial engines with custom fee and pricing tools.

Outcomes

  • The program automated 80% of manual logistics processing in the Land division.
  • After the ride-alongs, the vendor moved to a new device version and the data team fixed the failing customer records.
  • In Aviation, the new fee and pricing tools raised transaction processing speed by 50%.
