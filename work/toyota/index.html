Toyota North America

Delivery Lead on the Global Planning System: demand forecasting modernization across North America and Japan-based operations.

Jan 2024 to Jan 2025Plano, TXvia Aquent

The work

Context

Toyota's Global Planning System runs demand forecasting across North America and Japan-based operations. The modernization effort cut across supply planning, engineering, and business stakeholders on two continents, each operating on different timezones, delivery cadences, and decision rights.

What I did

  • Led delivery of demand forecasting modernization workstreams across the Global Planning System.
  • Coordinated cross-functional teams spanning North America and Japan-based operations on a shared delivery cadence.
  • Translated forecasting model behavior into stakeholder-readable narratives so supply planners could trust and adopt the new outputs.
  • Held the delivery rhythm across timezone-split teams through standups, demos, risk reviews, and stakeholder check-ins.

Outcomes

  • Kept the NA and Japan workstreams aligned on a single delivery cadence across the engagement.
  • Forecasting model narratives adopted by supply planners as the default explanation for model-driven decisions.
  • Delivered through the full engagement window (Jan 2024 to Jan 2025) with no missed major milestones.
