Toyota North America

Project Lead (consultant) for the team building the planner and supplier portal, within Toyota's North American planning-system modernization.

Jan 2024 to Jan 2025Plano, TXvia Aquent

The work

Context

I joined Toyota North America's planning-system modernization in 2024, during discovery. The goal was to move supply chain planners, who had done about 90% of their work in Excel for decades, onto a tool with machine-learning demand forecasts. McKinsey consultants, developers and architects built most of it, working alongside some Toyota architects and developers.

Planners did not trust the forecasts the portal displayed, and mostly checked them against their own Excel numbers. Much of the cause was organizational: the forecasting team had built ahead, and the Supply and Demand squads were not moving together.

What I did

  • Led the team building the planner and supplier portal: wrote the user stories and acceptance criteria, ran refinement and the agile ceremonies, and coordinated with a separate McKinsey-led team that developed the machine-learning forecasting.
  • Ran alignment sessions with product owners, business stakeholders and each team's McKinsey consultant to surface dependencies early and turn strategy decks into stories, a shared backlog and release roadmaps.
  • With agile coaches, trained about five supply chain planners to act as scrum masters.

Outcomes

  • We delivered a pilot MVP in about the first six months.
  • Issues and dependencies surfaced before development instead of at stakeholder demos.
  • The planners I trained were working as scrum masters when I left.
  • The tool was still a pilot when my contract ended in January 2025, since leadership changes had pushed the roadmap.
