Taas

Chhwela

Chicken 10.99 · Buff 14.99 · Mushroom 9.99

A traditional Newari dish, Chhwela features charred grilled meat, that is seasoned with a blend of spices, garlic, and herbs and then tempered with hot mustard oil.

About

Born out of a dream to bring the warmth of Newa culture to Dallas, The Bara is more than just a restaurant; it's a celebration of tradition and a journey through the culinary wonders of Nepal.
