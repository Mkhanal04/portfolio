What's in this demo

For business owners: what each part does, and what we would set up for you.

  • One room, one dayThree scenes follow the light. Yours would come from photos of your own installs.
  • See it in your roomSwatches and a time switch change the room. Your fabrics go in one settings file.
  • Product finderThree questions, then two or three suggestions, using your own product rules.
  • Catalog and searchFilters with counts. Search that handles curtains, drapes and typos. Any filtered view can be shared as a link.
  • List and estimateVisitors save products and see a price range for their windows, from your prices.
  • Booking requestChecks the ZIP code against your service area. On a live site it goes to your inbox or customer system.
  • AskAnswers only from your content, says it is automated, and hands off to a person. Questions it cannot answer become your list of content to add.
  • AccessibleWorks by keyboard and with screen readers. Shows still images when Reduce Motion is on.
  • What you would measureBooking requests, swatch use, finder completions and searches with no results. Cookie-free analytics.
  • PricesThe ranges come from published cost guides. Yours would replace them.
  • ReviewsReal client reviews, with permission, would sit between the finder and the questions.
  • ContactThe phone number is a placeholder from the fictional 555 range.
  • ImagesThe room is a generated illustration made for this concept. A few product photos are stand-ins.
